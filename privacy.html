Privacy policy

Namiluna: PCOS Tracker · effective October 25, 2026

Namiluna is a log for life with PCOS: periods, symptom check-ins, daily habits, supplements by name, weight and notes. We collect no data. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log, your plan and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Your device backups (iCloud or a computer) may include them, depending on your Apple settings.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, Namiluna reads and writes your menstrual flow and your weight in the Health app, with your permission. This data stays on your device, is never shared, and is never used for advertising or marketing. You can remove access at any time in Health › Sharing.

Doctor report

The PDF report is made on your iPhone and is shared only if you choose to send it from the iOS share sheet.

Reminders

Notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. No remote notification service is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, contact or payment details.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings › Erase all data. Deleting the app also deletes all its data. Periods and weights already saved in Apple Health stay there until you delete them in Health.

Not medical advice

Namiluna is for tracking and education. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, it is not a medical device, and it never suggests a medicine, a supplement or a dose. Talk to your doctor about your health.

Changes and contact
